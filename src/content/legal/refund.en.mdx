## 1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time on the billing page. Service continues until the end of the current billing period; no further charges are made.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

- **Within 7 days of payment** AND **no paid feature has been used yet**: full refund
- Service has a critical defect preventing normal use: prorated refund
- Other reasons (change of mind, partial use): refund not granted as a rule

## 3. How to Request a Refund

Email `support@ultracoach.kr`. We reply within 3 business days.

## 4. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed via the original payment method. Depending on the card issuer, processing takes 3–7 business days.
