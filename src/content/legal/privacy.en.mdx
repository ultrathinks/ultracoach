## 1. Personal Information We Collect

- Required: email, name, profile image (Google OAuth)
- For paid users: payment information via Toss Payments (we do not store card numbers directly)
- During Service use: interview audio and text answers, metrics extracted from camera (gaze, posture, facial expression), resume files

## 2. Purpose of Collection and Use

- Member identification and authentication
- Interview analysis and feedback
- Paid service billing
- Service improvement and statistical analysis

## 3. Retention and Use Period

All personal data and interview data are deleted immediately upon account deletion. Information that must be retained under applicable law is kept for the required period.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide personal data to third parties except for the following processors:

- OpenAI: API calls for interview answer analysis
- ElevenLabs: interviewer voice synthesis
- Simli: virtual interviewer video generation
- Toss Payments: payment processing

## 5. Use for Model Training

Your interview data is not used to train AI models by default. It may be used in anonymized form only if you explicitly opt in via the profile page.

## 6. Your Rights

- Right to access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Right to request processing suspension
- Right to delete your account (available immediately on the profile page)
